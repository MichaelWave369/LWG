# Style Profile: Soft Aurora

## Identity
Warm editorial SaaS: serif display on warm off-white, soft floating cards, gentle gradients used ONCE in the hero only.

## Colors
- bg `#F4F2EE`, surface `#FFFFFF`, text `#1A1A1C`, muted `#6E6E76`, accent `#5B5BF7`
- Gradients allowed exactly once: hero background, 30% opacity, blurred 80px.

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces 300-500, `clamp(2.75rem, 7vw, 6rem)`, italic allowed for one emphasis word
- Body: Inter 16px/1.7

## Signature elements
1. Cards float up 8px on hover with soft shadow growth (no borders)
2. One blurred gradient blob drifting 20s loop behind hero
3. Serif italic accent words in headlines

## Motion language
- Entrances: blur(8px) + fade + translateY(30px), 1.0s expo
- Stagger: 120ms
- Micro: buttons darken 5% on hover; links have accent underline

## FORBIDDEN
- ❌ dark mode  ❌ neon/glassmorphism  ❌ more than one gradient on the page
- ❌ sharp 0px corners (use 16-24px radius)  ❌ centered hero with two buttons
